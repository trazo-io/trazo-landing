# Step 3 photo brief — "Walk Into Renewal Ready"

Drop the file at `public/images/step-renewal-ready.jpg` and point
`components/how-it-works.tsx` step 3 at it. Nothing else changes: the card already
crops with `object-cover` and hovers with `scale-105`, both of which are correct for a
photograph.

## What the shot has to do

Steps 1 and 2 are warm photographic stock — interior/exterior golden light, a single
person mid-task, shallow depth of field, no text. Step 3 has to sit in that same
register or the section reads as broken. It also has to land the step's meaning:
**the moment the paperwork stops being a threat.**

## Subject — in priority order

1. **A grower and a certifier/inspector at a table**, paperwork between them, relaxed —
   not tense, not adversarial. This is the literal renewal moment and the strongest
   option. A tablet or laptop may be on the table but must not show a screen (see
   "Hard nos").
2. **A grower alone, confident, with a folder or binder**, farm office or kitchen table,
   window light, orchard visible outside. Reads as "ready" rather than "buried."
3. **A grower walking out toward the orchard with a folder under one arm.** Literal
   reading of "walk into renewal ready."

## Composition

- **Landscape, min 2000px wide**, ideally 2400×1600 or larger. The card renders roughly
  615×302 CSS px on desktop and switches to `aspect-[4/3]` below `lg`, so the subject
  must survive both a wide letterbox and a squarer crop. **Keep the subject centered and
  off the outer 15% on every edge.**
- Shallow depth of field, matching steps 1–2.
- Warm/neutral grade. Avoid heavy teal-orange.

## Context, since the ICP is a mid-size California wholesale orchard

Walnut, almond or citrus orchard if the outdoors is visible. Row crops and greenhouses
read as a different operation. Rolling green hills read European — that is exactly why
the current interim (`farm-field.jpg`) is a placeholder and not the answer.

## Hard nos

- **No fabricated UI.** Any screen in frame must be off, angled away, or genuinely
  showing Trazo. `step-signup.jpg` currently shows an AI-invented Trazo screen with a
  garbled wordmark and a leaf logo that is not ours — on a compliance product that is a
  credibility risk, and it should be replaced on the same pass.
- **No grocery store, no shopper, no retail shelf.** That is the pre-pivot story; the
  buyer page is a by-product of finishing a harvest, not the climax.
- **No USDA Organic seal, no certifier logo, no agency badge** on visible paperwork,
  clothing or signage. Only the USDA Organic seal is displayable by us and only where
  it is earned; a certifier's mark in a marketing photo implies an endorsement we do not
  have.
- **No legible text on documents in frame.** Blurred or angled is fine. Legible text
  invites the reader to check it, and anything we cannot substantiate becomes a claim.
- No drone/aerial hero shots — that is the interim's weakness, not a target.
- No hard hats, hi-vis, or industrial-processing settings.
